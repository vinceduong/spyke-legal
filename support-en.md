# Spyke Support

A question, an issue, or a suggestion about Spyke? Write to us — we usually reply within 48 hours.

## Contact

E-mail: [spyke.training@gmail.com](mailto:spyke.training@gmail.com)

## To help us help you faster

Please include in your message:

- your iPhone model and iOS version;
- a description of what happens (and what you expected);
- a screenshot or screen recording if possible.

## Account and data

- You can delete your account directly in the app: settings → Account & backup → Delete account.
