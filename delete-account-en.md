# Delete your Spyke account

Spyke is a workout tracking app published by Vincent Duong (independent developer). This page explains how to delete your Spyke account and the data associated with it.

## From the app

This is the fastest route: the account and data hosted by Spyke are deleted without waiting for external providers to finish processing.

1. Open Spyke.
2. Go to the Settings tab.
3. Tap Account & backup.
4. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

## By email

If you no longer have the app installed, or if you cannot access your account, email [spyke.training@gmail.com](mailto:spyke.training@gmail.com) with "Account deletion" as the subject, from the email address associated with your Spyke account.

Your request is handled within 30 days at most, usually within 48 hours. If the message does not come from the address linked to the account, you will be asked to confirm your identity before any deletion takes place.

## What is deleted

- Your online backup: every backup file stored on our servers.
- That backup's metadata: date, device name, app version and size.
- Your account and the associated sign-in identifier (Apple or Google), including your email address and your name.
- Cloud copies of your videos and thumbnails, and error reports linked to your account.
- PostHog usage analytics events, if you accepted analytics: account deletion starts a separate erasure request.

Permanently deleted data cannot be restored. PostHog erasure is asynchronous: the request is kept, retried after outages and verified after processing. Identifiers required for this follow-up are kept until verification, then for up to 90 days when your account has been deleted. Our providers' technical logs, described in the [privacy policy](./privacy-en), may persist temporarily.

## Erase usage analytics only

You can withdraw consent in Settings → Application → Improve Spyke to stop new measurements. To erase events already received without deleting your account, email [spyke.training@gmail.com](mailto:spyke.training@gmail.com) with "PostHog erasure" as the subject. After verifying your identity, we request erasure and keep a marker preventing collection from being reactivated unintentionally.

## What stays on your device

Your training history, routines, photos and videos are stored locally on your phone. This procedure does not erase them: they remain under your control. To remove them, uninstall the app.
